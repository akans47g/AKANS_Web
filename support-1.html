Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take to get my wedding card?
+
Your digital wedding invitation is ready almost instantly after completing your order. You'll receive a shareable link right away — no waiting at all.
Can I edit my invitation after sending it?
+
Yes! You can make unlimited edits right up until your wedding date. Changed the venue or timing? Just update it — all changes reflect instantly for your guests.
Is there a limit on how many guests I can share with?
+
No limit at all. One link, unlimited guests — no per-guest charges, ever. Share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, or anywhere you like.
What happens to my invitation after the wedding?
+
Your invitation stays publicly shareable for 30 days after your wedding, then automatically turns private to protect your privacy.
How do I add my own photos to the invitation?
+
You can upload your own couple photos as a slideshow or hero background when creating or editing your invitation. Contact us for help with this!
